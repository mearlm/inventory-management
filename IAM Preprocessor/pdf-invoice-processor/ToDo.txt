3. This also suggests a useful future summary field:
      "tax": Decimal(...)
      "taxable_base": Decimal(...) | None
      "tax_allocation_status": "KNOWN" | "UNKNOWN"
   For fully taxed invoices, taxable_base can be inferred from the summary. For partially taxed Yami invoices without item markers, it remains None.
   So I think the right principle is: extract the tax exactly, validate the invoice exactly, but do not invent the line/category allocation when the source does not support it. That is precisely the sort of thing your REVIEW state is for.
